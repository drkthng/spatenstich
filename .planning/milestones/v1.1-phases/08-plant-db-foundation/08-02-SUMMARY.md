### Migration 019 — Schema Foundation

**File:** `supabase/migrations/20260517000019_plants_and_companions.sql` (commit `257cc07`)

- **Section 1** — `plants` table with 30 columns: slug (UNIQUE), name_de + name_alt_de[] + name_botanical, family + category (CHECK 5-enum), spacing/depth, sun_requirement + water_needs (CHECK 3-enum each), climate zone min/max, 8 DOY ranges (sow_outdoor/indoor + plant + harvest start/end), days_to_harvest, nitrogen_fixing + perennial booleans, notes_de + icon_emoji, data_source, timestamps. 2 indexes (family, category). RLS: `plants_read_authenticated` SELECT-only to authenticated. NO write policies (service-role-only writes).
- **Section 2** — `plant_companions` with canonical `plant_a_id < plant_b_id` storage. Named CHECK `plant_companions_canonical_check`. UNIQUE (a, b) pair. relationship CHECK 3-enum. 2 indexes on a_id + b_id. Same read-only RLS policy.
- **Section 3** — DO-block invariants: asserts 2 tables exist, 2 RLS policies exist, ≥5 CHECK constraints exist (5 named: plants_category_check, plants_sun_requirement_check, plants_water_needs_check, plant_companions_relationship_check, plant_companions_canonical_check). Final `RAISE NOTICE 'migration_019 ok'`.

Migration is **COMMITTED but NOT pushed**. Plan 04 owns the autonomous 3-gate push.
