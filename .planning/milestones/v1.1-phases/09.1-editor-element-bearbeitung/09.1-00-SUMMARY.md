**1. [Rule 1 - Bug] Pre-existing TypeScript error in WebPlanEditor.tsx**
- **Found during:** Task 1 TSC gate
- **Issue:** `WebPlanEditor.tsx` line 157: `(e.target as HTMLElement) !== e.currentTarget` — TypeScript 5.8 correctly flags that `HTMLElement` and `EventTarget & SVGSVGElement` have no overlap (TS2367).
- **Fix:** Cast `e.target` to `EventTarget` (the common base type): `(e.target as EventTarget) !== e.currentTarget`.
- **Files modified:** `app/src/components/editor/web/WebPlanEditor.tsx`
- **Commit:** e06af87
